**The challenge:** Government advisories are published in incompatible formats across national boundaries. CERT-Bund uses CSAF, CERT-JP uses their own schema, and most CERTs publish free-text advisories. Covering global government intelligence is a manual, full-time effort.

**What you get:** Structured, queryable access to advisories from 20+ national CERTs and government cybersecurity agencies. Normalised into a common schema with cross-referenced CVE identifiers.
