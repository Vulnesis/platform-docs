The Vulnesis Vulnerability Database aggregates intelligence from 150+ sources across seven categories. Every record is tagged with its origin, linked to upstream advisories, and queryable through a single API.

| Category | Sources | Covers |
|---|---|---|
| [Authoritative vulnerability intelligence](/vdb-api/sources/vulnerability-intelligence/) | 23 | Core vulnerability databases and numbering authorities worldwide. |
| [Government and national cyber defence](/vdb-api/sources/government-cyber-defence/) | 23 | National CERTs and government cybersecurity agencies. |
| [Vendor and product security](/vdb-api/sources/vendor-product-security/) | 24 | Vendor PSIRTs, product advisories, and industry security bulletins. |
| [Supply chain and malware detection](/vdb-api/sources/supply-chain-protection/) | 10 | Malicious package identification and supply chain threat feeds. |
| [Exploit intelligence](/vdb-api/sources/exploit-intelligence/) | 21 | Exploit databases, PoC tracking, and attack framework modules. |
| [Threat observation and research](/vdb-api/sources/threat-observation/) | 9 | Honeypot data, scanning sightings, and threat research feeds. |
| [Ecosystem and patch intelligence](/vdb-api/sources/ecosystem-patch-intelligence/) | 47 | OS distributions, language ecosystems, and package-level fix tracking. |
